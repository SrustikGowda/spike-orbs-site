Spike Orbs privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The Spike Orbs app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Spike Orbs channel to publish that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the Spike Orbs channel and to read that channel's basic details and its uploaded videos, so it can confirm that a post went out.

What it stores

The app stores its access permission privately, and keeps a record of each published video's title and link. It does not collect, sell or share personal information about viewers.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can remove the app's access at any time from Google account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Spike Orbs YouTube channel.
